# 1. Purpose

This matrix records the current state of the BHIV humanoid mechanical design by separating:

* what CAD/design evidence exists;
* what dimensional information exists;
* what manufacturing information exists;
* what physical hardware evidence exists;
* what has actually been physically tested;
* what remains unavailable or unverified.

The matrix is intentionally evidence-based.

`CAD Exists` does not mean `Manufactured`.

`Manufactured` does not mean `Physically Tested`.

`Reference dimensions` do not automatically represent the final canonical robot.

# 7. As-Built Evidence Rule

A component shall only be changed from `Manufactured: Unknown` to `Yes` when physical evidence is available, such as:

* photograph of fabricated part;
* purchase/receipt evidence for purchased part;
* manufacturing record;
* vendor record;
* assembly photograph;
* physical measurement;
* inspection record.

A component shall only be changed from `Physically Tested: No Evidence` after an actual test record exists.
